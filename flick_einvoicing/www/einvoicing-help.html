    Getting started

    How UAE e-invoicing works

    An e-invoice is not a PDF sent to the customer. It is a structured document exchanged between accredited service providers on the Peppol network, with its tax data reported to the FTA. There is no clearance step: the FTA does not approve an invoice before it moves. Flick's guide calls this the five-corner model.

    Two tracks, finishing on their own

    • Reporting is your obligation to the FTA. Delivery is the exchange with your customer.
    • They run separately and can succeed or fail independently. Delivery counts as done only when the buyer's access point has confirmed it.
    • The app follows both for every invoice and shows them as two fields on the submission: Reporting and Delivery.

    What that means in ERPNext

    • Each invoice keeps its own number as the e-invoice number. Flick requires it to be unique for your participant, so naming series must never repeat a number.
    • A filed invoice is never edited or cancelled to correct it: you issue a credit note (or, for a higher amount, a debit note) instead.
    • Results arrive after the submit, within minutes. The invoice is booked in ERPNext at once, as always; e-invoicing never holds up your accounts.

    When you receive

    • Flick is also your corner 3: it receives the e-invoices your suppliers send to your Peppol ID.
    • Flick reports their receipt to the FTA for you. The app brings each one in as a record from which a draft purchase invoice is made for review.
    • B2C sales are out of e-invoicing scope today, which is why walk-in customers are skipped by default.
    1. Corner 1 · youERPNext invoiceSubmitted as usual. The app checks it, then sends it to Flick as JSON.
    2. Corner 2 · FlickValidates and rendersBuilds the PINT-AE UBL document, validates and signs it.
    3. Track 1 · reportingFTAFlick reports the tax data document to the FTA (corner 5).
    4. Track 2 · deliveryBuyer's providerFlick delivers the invoice over Peppol to the buyer's access point (corner 3).
    5. Corner 4 · buyerTheir systemReceives it. The buyer's provider confirms receipt and reports it too.

    Getting started

    What the app does

    The app adds e-invoicing to the documents your team already uses. Nobody fills in a separate e-invoice: Sales, POS and self-billed Purchase Invoices are sent when they are submitted, and received e-invoices arrive by themselves.

    Sending

    • Checks every invoice before it can be submitted and lists everything missing, in plain words.
    • Check with Flick: Flick's full validation on a saved draft, without filing anything.
    • Sends tax invoices, credit notes, out-of-scope documents and self-billed invoices, in AED or any other currency.
    • Sends at once (Live) or every 10 minutes (Send Every (Minutes) in the settings) (Batch), and retries by itself when Flick cannot be reached.

    Following up

    • An E Invoice Submission per invoice: its status, both tracks, the attempts, and the next thing to do.
    • A banner on every invoice that says what e-invoicing will do with it, did, or why it does not apply.
    • Status updates read from Flick every five minutes (or pushed by webhook).
    • Flick's PDF and the UBL XML attached to the invoice; the FTA and Peppol files a click away.

    Receiving

    • Invoices sent to your Peppol ID arrive as E Invoice Received records, matched to the supplier.
    • One click makes a draft purchase invoice, from your purchase receipt or order when the supplier quotes it.
    • The draft comes to the supplier's net, VAT and total exactly, and says where anything differs.
    • Item codes the supplier uses are remembered, so the next invoice matches by itself.

    Guidance and control

    • The Flick workspace: a dashboard of what was sent, what Flick accepted and what needs attention.
    • A setup checklist per company, and the E-Invoice Status report.
    • A log of every call to Flick, without credentials.
    • Rules that protect the filing: no cancelling of filed invoices, no automatic reissue, ever.

    • Sent as the delivery address when it is complete. On an export or an e-commerce supply it is required, and checked like the customer's.

    • Only EXW, FCA, FOB, CIF, DAP and DDP can go on a UAE e-invoice. Another Incoterm on the invoice stops the submit.

    • Flick accepts PDF, PNG, JPEG, CSV, XML, XLSX and ODS files. The app sends the file itself, embedded in the e-invoice.

    • The e-invoice states the exchange rate from the invoice, and the VAT and total in AED from ERPNext's company-currency totals; each line's AED amount too. Flick does not work them out.

    • Use a template mapped to Z (zero rated) for exported goods; the check makes sure ERPNext charges 0%.

    Cancelling and correcting

    Cancelling an invoice in ERPNext would not withdraw it from the FTA, so the app allows it only while nothing has been filed. Afterwards the correction is a credit note.

    What the app allows

    • No submission, or a submission that is Queued or Rejected: the invoice may be cancelled, and its submission is marked Cancelled.
    • A submission in Error that was never attempted: may be cancelled.
    • A submission in Error after an attempt: not yet. The attempt may have reached Flick: use Check on Flick on the submission first.
    • Accepted by Flick (Processing, Completed, or Failed but not released): never. Issue a credit note.
    • Released by Flick (both tracks failed for good): may be cancelled, and the submission is cancelled with it.

    Corrections, case by case

    • Wrong price or quantity, wrong VAT: a credit note for the wrong invoice, and a new invoice if needed.
    • Wrong buyer details: correct the customer; then a credit note and a new invoice, or Resubmit if Flick released the number.
    • Flick refused it (Rejected): nothing was filed. Fix the cause and Retry; or cancel the invoice and start again.
    • Amount too low: a debit note referencing the original.

    Discounts, charges and rounding

    The e-invoice states what ERPNext has. Where ERPNext's figures can't be stated as they are, the submit stops and says what to fix; the app never adjusts a figure itself.

    Discounts

    • A line's own discount shows as a price discount: the price-list price and the discount off it.
    • The invoice's Additional Discount goes as an allowance per VAT category (reason 95, Discount), unless the settings spread it over the lines or prices include tax: then it is folded into the line amounts. Totals are the same either way.
    • A negative Additional Discount raises the total, to round it up for instance. PINT-AE has no negative allowance, so it goes as a charge with the Reason Code for Negative Discounts (AJ unless changed).

    Charges

    • A charge in the taxes table, such as freight, needs its account mapped under Charge Accounts, charged Actual, with a reason code and a VAT category used by at least one line.
    • Every other taxes row must be VAT, on the net total or on a previous row. Deducted taxes can't be e-invoiced.

    Amounts

    • Money is stated in fils, two decimals, whatever the site keeps. Unit prices and quantities go with up to six decimals.
    • VAT is worked out per category, 5% of the standard-rated amount, half a fil rounded up, as Flick derives it. ERPNext's VAT must agree within one fil.

    Rounding and the amount due

    • The amount due is ERPNext's rounded total (or grand total when rounding is disabled). The difference from net plus VAT goes as a rounding amount, so the buyer owes exactly what your books say.
    • Commercial Rounding in System Settings rounds half a fil up, as Flick does. Banker's Rounding leaves an occasional VAT amount a fil apart: the settings warn.

    The most important table in the settings. PINT-AE needs each line's VAT category; ERPNext knows it only through the tax template. Map every template the company invoices with. There is no default: an unmapped template stops the submit.

    1. 1Template Type: Sales Taxes and Charges Template (the invoice's), Item Tax Template (a line's), or Purchase Taxes and Charges Template (self-billing only).
    2. 2Template: only this company's templates are offered.
    3. 3Tax Category: S, Z, E, O, AE or N. A line's Item Tax Template always wins over the invoice's template.
    4. 4Exemption Reason, required for E: one of the four FTA reasons (financial services, residential, bare land, local passenger transport), with optional wording.
    5. 5Reverse-Charge Goods, required for AE: the goods type. Reverse-charge lines also need a GTIN barcode on the item and the buyer's TRN.
    6. 6Map the purchase template too when you self-bill.
    7. 7Document-Level Charges: rows charged Actual in the taxes table, such as freight, are sent as charges.
    8. 8Each charge account with its UNCL 7161 reason code (FC is freight), optional wording, and VAT category. Every other taxes row must be VAT, on the net total or a previous row.

    The check behind it

    • Before submitting, the app compares each line's category with what ERPNext charged: S must carry exactly 5%; Z, E, O and AE must carry 0%. Any mismatch is named, line by line.
    • VAT on the e-invoice is worked out per category, 5% of the standard-rated amount, rounded half a fil up, and must agree with ERPNext's within a fil.

    Templates ERPNext created

    • ERPNext's own UAE Zero and Exempted item templates name accounts of their own, so on their own they leave the line charged 5%: add the account the invoice charges VAT to, at 0% (see Zero-rated and exempt item templates).
    • Disable templates the company never uses, Excise ones for instance, instead of mapping them.

    Seller, payee and tax representative

    Three collapsible sections, all optional. Flick fills in the seller from the profile you were onboarded with; anything set here overrides it on every invoice of the company.

    1. 1Seller Details: a trading name and a contact (name, phone, email) to state on your invoices. Your legal name, TRN, Peppol ID and address come from Flick's profile.

    When these apply

    • Leave them empty unless your agreement or your tax adviser says otherwise: an empty section sends nothing.
    • A filled section applies to every invoice of the company, so it is set here once rather than on each invoice.

    Addresses abroad

    • A tax representative's address may be outside the UAE. Like any foreign address on the e-invoice, it is sent with the Emirate Sent for Addresses Abroad.

    Units of measure

    The e-invoice states each line's unit as a UN/ECE Recommendation 20 code: H87 for a piece, HUR for an hour, KGM for a kilogram. The app loads every code PINT-AE allows into ERPNext's Code List; you say which code each of your UOMs stands for.

    1. 1The Code List UN/ECE Recommendation 20, created and kept up to date by the app on every install and migrate.
    2. 2The code's name, here piece.
    3. 3The code sent on the e-invoice: H87.
    4. 4Applies To: add a row for each UOM this code stands for (Link Document Type UOM, Link Name your UOM). Here, UOM Nos.

    How to set it up

    • Open Common Code, filter Code List UN/ECE Recommendation 20, search the code (H87, C62, HUR, KGM, MTR, LTR, XBX…), and add your UOM under Applies To.
    • Each UOM is linked to exactly one code. A UOM linked to two codes, or to none, is named when an invoice using it is submitted.
    • A code PINT-AE later drops stays on the site, because UOMs may link to it, but invoices using it are refused until the UOM is linked to another.

    Which code

    • H87 (piece) and C62 (one) are the usual codes for counted items. Agree with your accountant which you use, and use it consistently.
    • Packaging units are Recommendation 21 codes starting with X, for instance XBX for a box.

    Zero-rated and exempt item templates

    Lines in another VAT category than the invoice's carry an Item Tax Template mapped to that category. The template must set the account the invoice charges VAT to at 0%, or ERPNext still charges the line 5%.

    1. 1ERPNext's UAE template UAE VAT Zero, mapped to Z in Tax Treatments.
    2. 2Row 1, as ERPNext created it: its own account VAT Zero at 0%.
    3. 3Row 2, added: VAT 5%, the account the invoice's 5% row charges, at 0%. Without it the line is charged 5% and the submit stops, naming this account.

    Do the same for

    • UAE VAT Exempted, mapped to E with its exemption reason.
    • Any template you create for reverse charge (AE): the buyer accounts for that VAT, so ERPNext must charge 0%.

    How the app tells you

    • The draft's banner, Check with Flick and the submit all say, for the line: it is mapped as zero rated but ERPNext charges 5% VAT, and which account to add to which template.

    Glossary

    The terms below appear on the screens and in Flick's documentation. Flick's guide defines the e-invoice terms in full.

    TermMeaning
    FTAThe Federal Tax Authority, which receives the tax data of every e-invoice.
    FlickThe accredited service provider (access point) the app sends to. Your company is a participant on Flick, with a participant ID (a UUID).
    PeppolThe network e-invoices travel on. Every business on it has a Peppol ID; in the UAE it is 0235: followed by the 10-digit TIN (for a VAT-registered business, normally the first ten digits of its TRN).
    TRNThe 15-digit VAT registration number, kept in ERPNext's Tax ID field on companies, customers and suppliers.
    PINT-AEThe UAE profile of the Peppol invoice standard. It sets the fields, the code lists and the ibr-… business rules Flick validates against.
    Document identifierThe e-invoice number: the ERPNext invoice number. It must never be reused for another document.
    Document type380 tax invoice, 381 credit note, 480 and 81 the same when every line is exempt or outside the scope of VAT, 389 and 261 self-billed invoice and credit note.
    Tax categoryEach line's VAT treatment: S standard rated (5%), Z zero rated, E exempt (with a reason), O outside the scope of tax, AE reverse charge, N profit margin scheme.
    SubmissionThe app's record of one invoice's e-invoicing: E Invoice Submission, numbered EINV-year-number.
    TrackReporting (to the FTA) or delivery (to the buyer). Each has a status: processing, then reported or delivered, or failed, or not required.
    SettledFlick has finished with a track. A failed track that is not settled is still being retried by Flick.
    ReleasedBoth tracks failed and settled: Flick lets the number be used again, and only then may the invoice be resubmitted or cancelled.
    Pre-productionFlick's test environment, with a shared test participant. Nothing filed there reaches the FTA.
    Events feedFlick's list of status changes, which the app reads every five minutes. A webhook can push them instead.
    UBLThe XML format of the e-invoice. Flick builds it from what the app sends; you can download it from each submission.
    Tax Data DocumentThe tax data Flick reports to the FTA for each e-invoice (TDD).
    MLSMessage Level Status: the receipt the buyer's access point sends back. Delivery is done when it arrives.
    Predefined recipientA Peppol ID PINT-AE provides for buyers who can't receive over Peppol: the invoice is reported to the FTA only.

    Questions and answers

    Questions finance teams ask most, with the answer in short.

    Do we work differently?

    • No. Invoices are made and submitted as before. The banner tells you if something is missing; after the submit, the submission follows it.

    Can we invoice in dollars or euros?

    • Yes, in any currency. The e-invoice also states the exchange rate and the VAT and total in AED.

    What if Flick is down when we submit?

    • The submit goes ahead; the submission shows Error and is retried automatically.

    Can we cancel an invoice?

    • Only before it reached Flick, or once Flick released its number. Otherwise issue a credit note. See Cancelling and correcting.

    The buyer says they never got it.

    • Open the submission. Delivery delivered means the buyer's provider confirmed it; the Buyer's Receipt (MLS) is under Download. Not required: it was reported to the FTA only.

    How do we know the FTA has it?

    • The submission's Reporting track says reported. The Tax Data Document is under Download.

    A supplier calls a product something else.

    • Names are never used to match. Choose your item once on the draft; submitting remembers the supplier's code for it.

    Why does a foreign address carry Dubai?

    • Flick accepts only a UAE emirate on any address. The app sends the emirate the goods leave from.

    How many decimals?

    • Money in fils, two decimals. Unit prices and quantities up to six.

    Walk-in customers?

    • Consumer (B2C) sales are out of e-invoicing scope today and are not sent.

    Invoices dated before e-invoicing started?

    • They are never sent. A credit note for one names the original under Preceding Invoices on the E-Invoicing tab.

    Who do I ask when something fails?

    • Your system administrator. Give the invoice number and the submission's Next Action.
